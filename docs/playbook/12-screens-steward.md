# 12 — Steward, inspection and payment screens

**PROPOSED.** Apply shared conventions G from document 10; APIs/data/contracts/notifications are normative in documents 04–08. Every amount and settlement is fictional. No wallet/token/credit interface is introduced.

## W01 — My tasks

- **Access/route/layout:** W `/my/tasks`; G + across-project assignment list, five task-type tabs, due/status/supply readiness and next action.
- **Fields/actions/API:** project/type/status filter; A75 `assigned_to=me`→AssignmentSummary[]; Open assignment W02; no coordinator “complete as worker” action. No editable fields on list.
- **DB/chain:** read only; **no chain**. N02/N03 are shown through shared inbox, not generated by opening list.
- **Transitions/flows:** assigned/accepted/in_progress/submitted/verified_complete/changes_requested/rejected/cancelled; no assignments gets neutral empty state; supply pending explains plantation next step without hiding assigned work; retry safe on load error.
- **Tests:** UI-21, TASK-01–05, SEC-03: all task types, ownership, no cross-project leakage, shared claim IDs.

## W02 — Assignment details and contract obligations

- **Access/route/layout:** assigned W write-own, O/V/P scoped read `/assignments/:assignmentId`; G + task instructions, exact site/type/policy, supply summary, evidence checklist, timeline and component/milestone table.
- **Fields/actions/API:** A22→AssignmentDetail; Accept/Start A23 `{action}`→state; Submit evidence→W03; View claim→T08; Inspect due checkpoint→W04; View reward→W06; O cancel with reason A23. Unit/quantity/policy read-only once assigned.
- **DB/chain:** accept/start/cancel state/audit/outbox; no new chain milestone or authorization for Accept/Start. Assignment was registered on-chain by T04; cancellation queues project/assignment-related invalidation/ancillary audit as specified, and blocks new claims/settlement in DB.
- **Notifications/transitions:** N03 accept/cancel, N06 submit elsewhere. Assigned→accepted→in_progress; declined/cancel after submitted work routes to dispute, not silent deletion. Pending supply permits reading/start preparation, but submission clearly explains blocked quantity; stale ETag reloads.
- **Tests:** UI-22, TASK-02/03, SEC-04, FLOW-01: correct pinned contract, status gates and no reward on task checkbox.

## W03 — Work submission and correction

- **Access/route/layout:** assigned W `/assignments/:assignmentId/submit`; G + type-driven form, quantity/supply panel, map read context, upload/checklist and final exact-version summary. Correction banner links prior revision/reason.
- **Fields/validation:** work date, type-specific values (collection count/weight/source; nursery hours/batch; plantation count/species/cohort/supply; maintenance hours/area/subtype; monitoring visit/report); quantity/unit per task schema; site/policy fixed; evidence category sets, capture declarations, optional snapshot. Date within task/policy bounds, quantity <= assigned, plantation confirmed inventory, sealed valid set, no untyped JSON editing.
- **Buttons/API:** A22 bootstrap; A24 draft or A27 correction; A25 save draft; shared A29–A34 uploads/set/seal; Submit A28 exact digest/policy/acknowledgments→202 checks; A26/A62/A61 progress. Back preserves draft; no Verify/Authorize button.
- **DB/chain:** one shared Claim + revision, inventory reservations at submit, CheckRun/job/audit/outbox; commitSubject after valid inputs/dependencies. Failed upload/check not an on-chain approval. Corrected subject gets new version/commitment and independent review.
- **Notifications/transitions:** N06 acknowledgment, N07 blocked/error, N08 V handoff; draft→submitted/checking→review_ready or checks_blocked/error. Recoverable failure keeps draft; hard fail correction creates new version; duplicate click returns same resource; prior approved claim requires dispute/hold before replacement.
- **Tests:** UI-23, CHECK-01–04, SUPPLY-02–04, VERSION-01–04, EVID-01–08: typed forms across all task types, version integrity, race-safe supply, no fabricated approval.

## W04 — Inspection / independent visit recorder

- **Access/route/layout:** assigned collector/W records observation; V records independent report; scoped readers read-only `/milestones/:milestoneId/inspection`; G + milestone criteria/due/window, original cohort, context sufficiency, field counts, upload panel and revision history. `purpose=observation` versus `purpose=review_visit&review_assignment_id=...` is explicit validated mode, not arbitrary role override.
- **Fields/validation:** visit date/method, original accepted planting count read-only, live/dead/missing, plot/cohort, GPS/accuracy/source, notes, context status (`satellite_insufficient` allowed), snapshot ref?, evidence set, late reason when outside window. Primary census counts sum to original cohort. PLANTING visit uses accepted count/method rather than survival fields. All measurements/field report tied to exact target; no satellite-estimated count autofill.
- **Buttons/API:** A45/A22 criteria/context; A46 create purpose-tagged draft/A47 save/A48 submit→immutable inspection/check/review handoff; optional A64/A65 context capture; shared evidence APIs; V returns to T10 to decide the worker's exact subject, never their own work claim.
- **DB/chain:** observation/report revision and evidence refs/check operations; observation commitSubject for M3/M6, reviewer-visit evidence is bound into DecisionInput by A43 and does not replace worker/collector subject. Independent report submission alone sets no approved bit.
- **Notifications/transitions:** N12 observation ready, N13 only after review; due/overdue→observation_submitted→under_review→passed/failed through decision. Insufficient science leads to supported field path; bad/missing counts/evidence require correction; new inspection revision supersedes, not edits signed decision.
- **Tests:** UI-24, MON-01–06, REVIEW-02, CHAIN-05/06: 3/6 months, original denominator, field insufficiency route, late reason, observation/reviewer report separation and no self-approval.

## W05 — Reward components and eligibility list

- **Access/route/layout:** W own, P/O scoped and A redacted `/projects/:projectId/rewards`; G + assignment/component rows showing separate requirement, eligibility, human authorization, chain and simulation columns.
- **Fields/actions/API:** filters component/status/own worker; A49→ObligationSummary[]; Open W06; P/O Re-evaluate A50→Evaluation with exact dependency digest. No manual eligible toggle/rate edit.
- **DB/chain:** reevaluation appends deterministic evaluation under obligation lock; read does none. **No chain authorization** merely from eligibility computation; optional ancillary audit only.
- **Notifications/transitions:** N14 only changed evaluation; pending_requirements/eligible/ineligible/held/revoked. Missing M3 or M6 displays exact blocker and milestone link. Disabled completion/survival component shown not contracted; not silently an extra payable reward.
- **Tests:** UI-25, REWARD-01–06, POLICY-03: final mask all required, separate components, no duplicate obligation after revisions/retries, amounts server-calculated.

## W06 — Obligation detail and human authorization

- **Access/route/layout:** W own read, P decision, O/A scoped read `/rewards/:obligationId`; G + component/version and formula, exact prerequisite decisions/evidence links, amount PKR, conflicts/holds, four independent states, authorization drawer.
- **Fields/validation:** read fixed accepted quantity/rate/cap/rounding/currency and current eligibility digest; P mandatory reason and expected amount acknowledgment, no editable recipient/rate/amount. Reject with reason; conflict blocked even if P also has worker/verifier role.
- **Buttons/API:** A51 detail; Re-evaluate A50; Authorize A52→human approved_pending_chain and operation; View authorization W07; dispute A68. For explicit human rejection, A52 accepts `outcome="rejected"` with reason and creates a rejected authorization history without chain authorization.
- **DB/chain:** lock obligation/dependencies, freeze eligibility/amount/payee; one active authority; authorizeReward sent as P-bound signer after required commitments confirmed. Contract independently checks mask/current versions/threshold/integer amount and conflicts; no token transfer.
- **Notifications/transitions:** N15 approval/rejection; N16 confirmed authority/N17 failure; eligible remains separate from approved_pending_chain/authorized. Premature/current-input mismatch 409; corrected dependency reevaluates under same obligation; rejected authority may be requested again with distinct audit attempt, never changes rates.
- **Tests:** UI-26, REWARD-02–06, PAY-01–03, CHAIN-05–08: missing milestone denied direct RPC, exact amount and self-authorizer denied, no settlement after mere human approval.

## W07 — Authorization and simulated settlement dispatch

- **Access/route/layout:** P action/W own read/O/A scoped `/authorizations/:authorizationId`; G + immutable human approval, prerequisite/chain receipt panel, blockers, simulation acknowledgment, attempt list and Cancel/hold drawer.
- **Fields/validation:** read-only amount/payee/currency/eligibility; checkbox “This records a fictional simulated settlement; no money is transferred”; cancel reason. Dispatch only confirmed canonical authority, no current hold, isExecutable true, no settled/unknown obligation outcome.
- **Buttons/API:** A53→AuthorizationDetail; Simulate A55 `{acknowledge_simulated:true}`→202 attempt; Cancel A54→hold/cancel command; operation A61; attempt link W08. Duplicate action reuses idempotency and stable obligation economic key.
- **DB/chain:** enqueue durable simulator attempt after locked recheck; worker uses fresh contract isExecutable, simulator receipt then recordSimulatedSettlement. Cancel immediately holds DB, queues cancelAuthorization; cannot erase settled receipt. No browser signing or real financial API.
- **Notifications/transitions:** N15 cancel, N18 queued/outcome, N17 chain issues. Human approved + chain pending disables dispatch. Unknown/out-of-canonical-chain outcome holds and explains reconciliation. A cancelled unexecuted authorization may be replaced only after no simulator success/unknown exists; obligation remains same.
- **Tests:** UI-27, PAY-01–06, REC-03–06, CHAIN-07/08/11: simultaneous clicks/queued dispute/reorg/unknown outcome cannot simulate twice.

## W08 — Simulated settlement receipt / failure

- **Access/route/layout:** W own/P scoped/O summary/A redacted `/settlements/:attemptId`; G + prominent SIMULATED badge, frozen obligation/authority/amount, durable simulator reference, attempt timestamps/failure and chain-audit status timeline.
- **Fields/actions/API:** read-only receipt; A56→SettlementAttempt, A53 authority; Refresh; View audit S08; retry only via P W07 after known failure (same obligation key), unknown links to S10 for reconciliation; open dispute A68 when authorized. No fake bank transfer receipt/upload or wallet balance.
- **DB/chain:** read none; retry/hold through owning endpoints only. Receipt already exists before settlement audit tx, so chain-audit pending can coexist with simulated_settled; blockchain cannot prove external payment.
- **Notifications/transitions:** N18 outcome generated by job, N19 dispute. queued/processing/simulated_settled/failed/unknown/held displayed literally; success never renamed real-paid. Failure reason safe/retryable; prior attempts retained; settled then reversed verification marked disputed without deleting receipt or inventing refund.
- **Tests:** UI-28, PAY-04–06, NOTIF-03, REC-05: separate settlement/audit statuses, stable receipt idempotency, worker privacy and explicit fiction.
